Crew a huge machine across a dead surface with your friends, then climb down into an earth that runs on its own rules. Three mockups follow, one for each place you spend time: on the surface, below it, and inside your own fragile body.

Surface · cross-section

The Crawler, anchored over a shaft

Two crew hold the machine while two go down. Every station only works with a body standing at it, so Tal is on the radio, Ines is on the winch, and nobody is driving. The delve team below sees only what their lamps and the glowing fungi show them.

Not to scale. Placeholder art. Rooms are bolt-in modules; the layout is one possible build.

Below · live simulation

A cave that runs on rules

Every pixel here is one cell of material, updated by simple local rules. Pick a material and paint with it; your lamp follows the pointer. Jun stands at the foot of the winch shaft with a helmet lamp. Nothing below is scripted.

Gas detector
  • Gas pocketFind the faint purple methane trapped under a ceiling and put a spark to it.
  • Grey laceBurn the pale mycelium. It bursts into spores that drift and seed new colonies on wet rock.
  • AcidPour acid on rock. It eats through and gives off CO₂, which is heavier than air and pools in low tunnels.
  • Lava and waterPour water into the lava channel. It flashes to steam and leaves black glass behind.
